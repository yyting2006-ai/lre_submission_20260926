\vspace{1em}
\noindent Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``ExecKG: A source-anchored executable grammar resource for GF~0025--2021,'' as a full-length paper.
The paper presents a resource that compiles all 572 grammar points of the Chinese national standard into 646 diagnostic units, each with a source-linked profile, a runtime binding, and contract tests.
It separates inventory-wide compilation from behavioral validation and reports benchmark performance on 3{,}000 supplied candidates plus transfer to 244 author-written candidates.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere.
Author-created tables are offered under CC~BY~4.0 and the code under the BSD 3-Clause License.
The review package, named \texttt{ExecKG\_LRE\_supplement\_20260926.zip}, contains identifiers, page anchors, hashes, rule traces, predictions, compiler inputs, and verification scripts.
The wording of the standard and the handbooks, and the benchmark sentences, remain with the rights holders.
